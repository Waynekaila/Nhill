Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions freelancers, shopkeepers and SMEs ask. Another question? Write to us.

Getting started

How long does it take to get started?

About half a day: installing the applications, importing your products from a CSV file or a spreadsheet, training the people who will use Nhill. During the assisted trial we do it with you.

How much does Nhill cost?

Nhill is a subscription per establishment: your users, tills and warehouses are included according to the plan you choose, with no licence to pay for each employee. You start with an eight-week assisted trial, free and with no commitment. For a proposal suited to your business, write to the sales team.

In which currency am I billed?

In the currency stated on your written proposal, for example US dollars, euros or Congolese francs, with the plan and duration you choose. The amount you pay is the one in the proposal, in that currency, and the payment methods available in your country are set out there.

In which countries can Nhill be used?

Anywhere you have an Internet connection. The interface and documents are in English and French, your documents can be issued in ten currencies, and you set your own tax rates and legal identifiers. A detailed tax profile is ready for the DRC, and an adaptable commercial invoice is used in other countries.

Why choose Nhill rather than an ERP such as Odoo?

Because Nhill brings together from day one what a small business uses every day: sales, till, stock, purchases, expenses and payments, already connected. You get started in about half a day, with one subscription per establishment and a team that supports you. See the comparison.

What equipment do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or Linux computer for Nhill Business and Nhill POS; Nhill Business also runs on Android 7 or later phones and tablets. For the till: a receipt printer or a standard printer, and a USB barcode scanner if you like.

Is Nhill in French and English?

Yes: the interface, invoices, tickets and reports exist in both languages, and each user chooses their own.

Day to day

How does Nhill work with my Internet connection?

Nhill works online: every sale is saved in real time on a secure server, and you follow your business from anywhere, on your computer or phone. A mobile connection (3G, 4G) is enough. If the network drops during a sale, it is sent again without creating a duplicate.

Can I work in several currencies?

Yes. Each document has its currency, among ten: USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, XAF, XOF, CDF, KES, RWF and ZAR. Amounts are computed exactly, with no hidden rounding, and the day's exchange rate is saved with each foreign-currency document.

Are Mobile Money payments supported?

Yes. Each Mobile Money payment is recorded with its amount and reference, on a till ticket or an invoice, next to cash, card and bank transfer. The till closing report shows your takings method by method.

What happens when an employee leaves?

You disable their access in Settings › Team; their sessions are closed. Their past actions stay in the activity log.

Invoices and obligations

How does Nhill fit my country's tax rules?

Your tax rates and legal identifiers appear on every document. If your country requires a fiscal device or approved invoicing, such as the DGI standardised invoice in the DRC, your official documents are still issued by that tool and Nhill keeps their reference, to link each sale to its official document. Your accountant confirms the mentions required in your country.

Does Nhill prepare my accountant's work?

Yes. Sales, payment and expense journals, customer receivables and amounts to pay export to CSV and PDF, ready to hand over to your accountant.

Data and security

Who can see my data?

You and the people you invite, according to their role. Each establishment's data is kept apart from the others and travels encrypted. The Nhill team does not look at your customers, products or invoices; it sees overall usage figures to run the service.

I forgot my password: what do I do?

If you are an employee, ask the administrator of your establishment: in Settings › Team they give you a temporary password, and you choose your own at your next sign-in. If you are the owner or an administrator, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen: you receive a one-time code by e-mail, valid for a few minutes.

Can I get my data back if I stop?

Yes. CSV and PDF exports remain possible at any time, even if your subscription is suspended.
