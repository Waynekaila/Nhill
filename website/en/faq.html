Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions freelancers, traders and SMEs ask. Another question? Write to us.

Getting started

How long does it take to get started?

About half a day: installing the applications, importing your products from a CSV file or a spreadsheet, training the people who will use Nhill. During the assisted trial, we do it with you.

How much does Nhill cost?

Nhill is used by subscription, per business: your users, tills and warehouses are included according to the chosen plan, with no licence to pay for each employee. You start with a free 30-day assisted trial, with no commitment. For a proposal suited to your business, write to the sales team.

In which currency am I billed?

In the currency stated on your written proposal, for example the dollar, the euro or the Congolese franc, with the chosen plan and duration. The amount you pay is the one in the proposal, in that currency, and the payment methods available in your country are specified there.

In which countries can Nhill be used?

Wherever you have an Internet connection. The interface and documents are in English and French, your documents are issued in 36 currencies, and you set your tax rates, legal identifiers and notices. A settings profile is provided for the DRC; in other countries, the commercial invoice adapts to your settings. Your accountant confirms the notices your country requires.

Why choose Nhill over an ERP such as Odoo?

Because Nhill brings together from day one what a small business uses every day: sales, till, stock, purchases, expenses and payments, already linked. You get started in about half a day, with one subscription per business and a team that supports you. See the comparison.

What equipment do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or Linux computer for Nhill Business and Nhill POS; Nhill Business also runs on Android 7 or later phones and tablets. For the till: a receipt printer or an ordinary printer, and, if you wish, a USB barcode scanner.

Is Nhill in English and French?

Yes: the interface, invoices, receipts and reports exist in both languages, and each user chooses theirs.

Day to day

How does Nhill work with my Internet connection?

Nhill works online: every sale is recorded in real time on a secure server, and you follow your business from anywhere, on a computer or a phone. A mobile connection (3G, 4G) is enough. If the network drops during a sale, you confirm it again: it is never counted twice.

Can I work in several currencies?

Yes. Each document has its currency, among 36 currencies identified by their ISO 4217 code: USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, XAF, XOF, CDF, NGN, KES, ZAR and others. Amounts are computed exactly, with no hidden rounding, and the day's exchange rate is recorded with each document in a foreign currency.

Are Mobile Money payments supported?

Yes. Each Mobile Money payment is recorded with its amount and reference, on a receipt or an invoice, alongside cash, card, bank transfer and cheque. The till closing report shows your takings method by method.

Can the till issue an invoice?

Yes. For each sale, the cashier chooses a receipt or an A4 invoice in the customer's name. The invoice is the same as in Nhill Business: same numbering, same notices, same layout.

What happens when an employee leaves?

You disable their access in Settings › Team; their sessions are closed. Their past actions stay in the activity log.

Invoices and obligations

How does Nhill fit my country's tax rules?

Your tax rates, legal identifiers and notices appear on every document. If your country requires a fiscal device or approved invoicing, such as the standardised invoice in the DRC, your official documents are still issued by that tool and Nhill keeps their reference, to link each sale to its official document. Nhill is not itself an approved invoicing device. Your accountant confirms the notices your country requires.

Does Nhill prepare my accountant's work?

Yes. Sales, payments and expenses journals, customer receivables and amounts payable export to CSV and PDF, ready to send to your accountant.

Data and security

Who can see my data?

You and the people you invite, according to their role. Each business's data is kept apart from the others and travels encrypted. The Nhill team does not look at your customers, products or invoices; it sees overall usage figures to run the service, and each consultation appears in your activity log.

Where is my data hosted?

On a server located in France, in the European Union, rented from the hosting provider Hostinger. The data is backed up every day, encrypted.

I forgot my password: what should I do?

If you are an employee, ask your business administrator: in Settings › Team, they give you a temporary password, and you choose your own at the next sign-in. If you are an owner or administrator, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen: you receive a one-time code by e-mail, valid for a few minutes.

Can I get my data back if I stop?

Yes. CSV and PDF exports remain available at any time, even if your subscription is suspended, and the account holder can download the full archive of their data in one go.
