What Nhill changes in your day

Four moments every small business knows, and what happens with Nhill.

  • In the evening, the cash book does not add up, and nobody knows where the difference comes from.

    Every sale carries the cashier's name. At closing, Nhill works out the expected cash and records the difference. You know which session, which day, which person.

  • A stock-out is discovered in front of the customer.

    Stock moves with every sale and every purchase received. An alert flags the products that fall below their threshold, warehouse by warehouse.

  • Customer credit is tracked from memory or in a notebook.

    Every invoice keeps its balance. You see what each customer owes you, in the currency of their invoice, and part payments are matched to the right invoice.

  • You cannot be away without losing track of the shop.

    You follow everything remotely. Dashboard, sales per employee and activity log, from your computer or your Android phone.

Stay in control, even when away

Each employee has their own access and a suitable role: cashier, seller, accountant, manager. A large discount or a void needs a manager's approval.

Your business in your pocket

Nhill Business also runs on Android phones and tablets. Today's takings, unpaid invoices, stock alerts: you check them wherever you are, and you can make an invoice on the spot at a customer's, then send it by e-mail or messaging.

What you enter on the phone is visible at the office straight away, and the other way round: both work on the same customers, products and stock.

Built to sell anywhere, in several currencies

Nhill follows the way small businesses really work: customers who pay in several ways, sometimes in several currencies, at the counter as well as on the move.

36 currencies, exact amounts
Each document has its currency: dollar, euro, pound sterling, Swiss franc, CFA francs, Congolese franc, naira, Kenyan shilling, dirham, rand and others. Amounts are computed exactly, the day's rate is recorded with the document, and your totals stay presented currency by currency.
