What Nhill does for you

Applications connected to the same account: Nhill Business to manage, Nhill POS to sell at the counter, and your figures in one place.

  • Sales and invoicesQuotes, purchase orders, invoices and credit notes numbered automatically, as PDF, in French or English. Each customer's balance is up to date.
  • Cash register (Nhill POS)Opening float, sales with several payment methods, change, returns, 58 or 80 mm receipts, closing with variance and X and Z reports.
  • StockQuantities only move through recorded movements: sales, received purchases, justified adjustments. Alerts flag products below their threshold.
  • Payments and expensesMoney received, supplier payments and overheads with their receipts. Cash, card, Mobile Money, transfer: each payment is attached to its document.
  • Team and traceabilityEach employee has their own access and role: cashier, salesperson, accountant, manager. The activity log shows who did what, and when.
  • ReportsSales journal, payments, expenses, sales by product, customer receivables, amounts to pay: on screen, as PDF or CSV for your accountant.

The register closes on figures, not on doubt.

At the end of the day, the cashier counts the drawer. Nhill computes the expected cash from sales, returns and cash in and out, then records the variance. The manager sees it; the Z report keeps it.

A discount above the limit, a return or a void needs a supervisor's approval, with their own password.

Designed to work in the DRC

  • Two languagesInterface, invoices, receipts and reports in French or English, as each user prefers.

What to know before you start

Nhill needs an Internet connection. There is no offline mode: without a network, the register cannot record a sale. A mobile connection is enough.

Nhill is not a standardised invoicing system homologated by the DRC tax authority (DGI). If your business is VAT-registered, your standardised invoices are still issued by your electronic fiscal device; Nhill is for managing and tracking, and lets you record the reference of the standardised invoice.

Mobile Money payments are recorded by hand after checking on the phone or terminal; Nhill is not connected to the operators.
