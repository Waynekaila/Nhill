Forms of partnership

Referral
You introduce Nhill to businesses in your network. If they become customers, a commission agreed in advance is paid to you. Written referral agreement.
Installation and support
IT providers: you install Nhill, import the data and provide first-level support, paid by your customers or by Nhill Solution according to the agreement.
Training
Trainers, business schools, business centres: you train users on Nhill with our materials.
Entrepreneur support
Incubators, trade associations, SME support organisations: you offer Nhill to the businesses you support, for example as part of the free assisted trial.
Reference user
A business that uses Nhill and agrees, if it wishes, to welcome other entrepreneurs to share its experience.

Every partnership rests on a written agreement: commissions, responsibilities, confidentiality and termination terms are set before starting, and a partnership is only announced publicly with your agreement.
