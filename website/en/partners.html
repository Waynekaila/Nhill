Let's work together with entrepreneurs

Nhill is looking for a few partners who know shopkeepers and SMEs and can advise, equip or train them.

Forms of partnership

  • ReferralYou introduce Nhill to businesses in your network. If they become customers, a commission agreed in advance is paid to you. Written referral agreement.
  • Installation and supportIT providers: you install Nhill, import data and provide first-level support, paid by your customers or by Nhill depending on the agreement.
  • TrainingTrainers, business schools, business centres: you train users on Nhill with our materials.
  • Entrepreneur supportIncubators, professional associations, SME support organisations: you offer Nhill to the businesses you follow, for example through the pilot programme.
  • Reference userA business that uses Nhill and agrees, after the pilot and only if it wishes, to talk about it with other shopkeepers.

No partnership is announced publicly without a signed agreement. Commissions, responsibilities, confidentiality rules and termination conditions are set in writing before anything starts.
