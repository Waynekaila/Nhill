Nhill in your line of business

Four ways of working, and how Nhill fits in. These scenarios describe the real features of the application; the demo then happens on your own products.

A shop with a till and sales staff

Your day today. You are not always at the shop. In the evening you count the money and compare it with a notebook.

With Nhill. Each salesperson takes payments on Nhill POS with their own account. At closing they count the drawer; Nhill computes the expected cash and records the variance. You check the session and the Z report in Nhill Business, even from your phone.

What reassures you. A large discount or a void needs a supervisor's approval, and the activity log keeps every operation.

A hardware store or depot selling at the counter and on invoice

Your day today. Walk-in customers pay at the counter. Building sites and companies buy on a quote, then an invoice, and pay in instalments, in francs or dollars.

With Nhill. The counter runs on Nhill POS; business customers go through quote, invoice and partial payments in Nhill Business. Stock is the same for both, and customer receivables are shown per currency.

What saves you time. Importing the catalogue as CSV at the start, low-stock alerts before the stock-out, transfers between warehouses.

See Nhill for a hardware store or depot

A service provider or sole trader

Your day today. Quotes and invoices made in a word processor, payments followed from memory.

With Nhill. A simple space for one person: quotes and invoices as PDF in your name, payments received, expenses with receipts, and the list of what each customer owes you. On a computer or an Android phone.

See Nhill for a service provider

An SME with several employees and an accountant

Your day today. Several people sell and buy; the accountant receives the documents late.

With Nhill. Each employee has a suitable role: sales, manager, accountant reading the reports. Reports download as CSV and PDF, and the activity log shows who issued, collected or changed what.

What helps you grow. Several tills, several warehouses, and employees you add as you hire, according to your plan.

VAT-registered business? Your standardised invoices are issued by your electronic fiscal device; Nhill manages your sales, stock and payments, and keeps the reference of each standardised invoice.
