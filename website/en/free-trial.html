Questions about the trial

Is it really free?

Yes. During the trial, you pay neither for access nor for support, and no payment details are asked.

And after the trial?

You decide. The terms to continue are presented to you in writing before the end of the trial, with a plan suited to your business. You only pay after accepting a proposal, and in any case you keep the export of your data.

Who sees my data?

You and the people you invite. The Nhill team does not read your invoices, customers or products; it sees overall usage figures, and each consultation appears in your activity log. See the privacy policy.

Can I stop along the way?

Yes, at any time, by letting us know. You keep the full export of your data.
