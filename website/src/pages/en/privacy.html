Privacy policy

Version of 28 October 2026

This policy explains which personal data Nhill Solution processes, why, for how long, with whom, and how to exercise your rights. It covers the nhill.online website, the requests you send us, our meetings and the Nhill Business, Nhill POS and Nhill Console applications.

Who is responsible

Nhill Solution, with its head office in Kinshasa (Democratic Republic of the Congo), is responsible for the processing described here. Contact for any question about your data: info@nhill.online.

Data collected by this website

  • Forms (demo, free assisted trial, partnership, question): name, organisation, e-mail, phone, city, sector, number of users, message and your consent choices. We use them to answer your request and, only if you agreed, to send you news from Nhill, at most once a month.
  • Abuse protection: a non-reversible fingerprint of your IP address, computed when you send a form, to limit repeated submissions.
  • No cookies, no trackers: this website sets no cookie, uses no audience measurement or advertising tool, and loads no resource from another website.

Data collected outside the website

  • Meetings and referrals (visit, trade fair, referral by a partner): we note your name, position, business, commune, the contact details you give us and what you agree to receive, to follow up on your interest. We send you commercial messages only with your consent.
  • Testimonials, photos and videos: only with your written consent, given for each use, on content you have read. You can withdraw it at any time.

Why, and on what basis

Answering your request, organising a demo or a trialSteps you asked for before a possible contract.
Following up on a meeting or a referralOur legitimate interest in answering the interest you expressed; your consent for commercial messages.
Publishing a testimonial, a photo or a videoYour written consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
Sending you news from NhillYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
Protecting the website against abuseOur legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.
Providing the Nhill service to subscribing businessesThe contract with the business.

Data processed in the Nhill applications

The data a business records in Nhill Business and Nhill POS (customers, products, documents, payments, employees) belongs to that business. Nhill Solution processes it on the business's behalf, as a processor, under its service contract. We also process, on our own behalf, the data needed to manage accounts and subscriptions (name, e-mail, role, subscription history).

The Nhill Solution team does not look at the businesses' customers, products or documents. It sees overall usage figures per business (number of documents, volumes per currency, active users) to run the service and support, and each consultation appears in the business's activity log.

For how long

  • Requests received through the website: deleted automatically 12 months after they were sent.
  • IP address fingerprints: 30 days.
  • Contacts noted at a meeting or through a referral: 12 months after the last exchange, unless a subscription follows.
  • Testimonials, photos and videos: while the content is used; the signed consent is then kept as proof while a claim remains possible.
  • Agreement to receive news: recorded with your request, it ends as soon as you withdraw it and at the latest when the request is deleted.
  • A business's data: for the term of the contract, then 90 days to let it export the data, before deletion, unless the law requires keeping it.
  • Technical logs of the Nhill service server (IP addresses, dates, requests): 90 days at most.

Who receives your data

  • The members of the Nhill Solution team who handle your request or provide support, within the limits of their role.
  • Our hosting provider, Hostinger International Ltd (Cyprus), which provides the server of the Nhill service, located in France.
  • The provider that delivers our e-mails, for the messages we send you.

We do not sell your data and do not pass it to any advertiser.

Where the data is

The data of the Nhill service is hosted on a server located in France, in the European Union, and backed up every day in encrypted form. The Nhill Solution team, based in Kinshasa, accesses it remotely, over encrypted connections, for operations and support.

Security

Encrypted exchanges (HTTPS), each business's data kept apart from the others in the database, passwords stored as hashes, team access limited and logged, encrypted backups.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, erasure or portability, restriction of its use, object to its use and withdraw your consent to news at any time, by writing to info@nhill.online. We answer within one month. If you use Nhill within a business, first send your request to that business: it decides how its data is used, and we help it answer you.

You can also contact the data protection authority of your country, for example, if you live in the European Union, the one of your Member State.

Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes; the version date is shown at the top of the page. Significant changes are notified to subscribing businesses.
