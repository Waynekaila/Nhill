Access to the applications and pricing

During the pilot programme Nhill is free. Paid plans will be published here once they are set.

Pricing

The prices of the paid plans are not set yet. They will be based on the feedback from the pilot programme and published on this page, in Congolese francs and US dollars, before any billing.

Nhill works by subscription per establishment. Plans will differ by the number of users, registers and warehouses. A 30-day free trial is built into the application.

Downloads

The version 2.0.0 installers are given to pilot participants, with their SHA-256 checksums and the installation procedure. They will be published here after they have been verified on each platform.

You need: an Internet connection, a computer running Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or Linux, or a tablet running Android 7.0 or later; for the register, a receipt printer or an ordinary printer (the receipt is a 58 or 80 mm wide PDF). Precise minimum configurations will be confirmed during the pilot.
