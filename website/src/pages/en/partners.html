Forms of partnership

  • ReferralYou introduce Nhill to businesses in your network. If they become customers, a commission agreed in advance is paid to you. Written referral agreement.
  • Installation and supportIT providers: you install Nhill, import data and provide first-level support, paid by your customers or by Nhill depending on the agreement.
  • TrainingTrainers, business schools, business centres: you train users on Nhill with our materials.
  • Entrepreneur supportIncubators, professional associations, SME support organisations: you offer Nhill to the businesses you follow, for example through the free assisted trial.
  • Reference userA business that uses Nhill and agrees, after the pilot and only if it wishes, to share its experience with other entrepreneurs.

Every partnership rests on a written agreement: commissions, responsibilities, confidentiality and termination conditions are set before anything starts, and a partnership is only announced publicly with your agreement.
