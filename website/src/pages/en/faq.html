Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions freelancers, shopkeepers and SMEs ask. Another question? Write to us.

Getting started

How long does it take to get started?

About half a day: installing the applications, importing your products from a CSV file or a spreadsheet, training the people who will use Nhill. During the assisted trial we do it with you.

How much does Nhill cost?

Nhill is a subscription per establishment; the plan depends on how many users, tills and warehouses you need. The eight-week assisted trial is free. For a proposal suited to your business, write to the sales team.

In which currency am I billed?

The written proposal states the plan, the duration and the billing currency, for example US dollars, euros or Congolese francs. You pay the amount stated in that currency: no conversion is made without you knowing. The available payment methods depend on your country and are set out with the proposal.

In which countries can Nhill be used?

Anywhere with an Internet connection: the interface is in English and French, and your documents can be issued in ten currencies. Nhill offers a detailed tax profile for the DRC and a generic commercial invoice for other countries, with your own tax rates; check your country's mandatory mentions with your accountant.

Does Nhill replace an ERP such as Odoo?

Not entirely: a modular ERP also covers the general ledger, manufacturing or human resources. Nhill focuses on sales, the till, stock and payments, with one subscription per establishment. See the comparison.

What equipment do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or Linux computer for Nhill Business and Nhill POS; Nhill Business also runs on Android 7 or later phones and tablets. For the till: a receipt printer or a standard printer, and a USB barcode scanner if you like.

Is Nhill in French and English?

Yes: the interface, invoices, tickets and reports exist in both languages, and each user chooses their own.

Day to day

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Nhill records everything on a secure server, which lets you follow your business remotely; without a connection you cannot sell or invoice. A mobile connection (3G, 4G) is enough. If the server's answer is lost during a sale, the sale can be sent again without creating a duplicate.

Can I work in several currencies?

Yes. Each document has its currency, among ten: USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, XAF, XOF, CDF, KES, RWF and ZAR. Amounts are computed exactly, with no hidden rounding, and the day's exchange rate is saved with each foreign-currency document.

Are Mobile Money payments supported?

Yes, as a payment method: you record the amount and the reference after checking them on the phone or terminal, on a till ticket or an invoice. Nhill is not directly connected to the operators.

What happens when an employee leaves?

You disable their access in Settings › Team; their sessions are closed. Their past actions stay in the activity log.

Invoices and obligations

Is Nhill certified till or invoicing software?

No, in no country. Nhill is management software. Where the law requires a certified till, e-invoicing or a fiscal device, those documents are still issued by the approved tool. In the DRC, for example, VAT-registered businesses issue their standardised invoices with their electronic fiscal device: Nhill keeps their reference, and its documents carry the mention "management document".

Does Nhill keep my accounts?

Nhill does not keep the general ledger, but it produces the journals and exports (CSV, PDF) your accountant needs: sales, payments, expenses, receivables and payables.

Data and security

Who can see my data?

You and the people you invite, according to their role. Each establishment's data is kept apart from the others and travels encrypted. The Nhill team does not look at your customers, products or invoices; it sees overall usage figures to run the service.

I forgot my password: what do I do?

If you are an employee, ask the administrator of your establishment: in Settings › Team they give you a temporary password, and you choose your own at your next sign-in. If you are the owner or an administrator, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen: you receive a one-time code by e-mail, valid for a few minutes.

Can I get my data back if I stop?

Yes. CSV and PDF exports remain possible at any time, even if your subscription is suspended.
