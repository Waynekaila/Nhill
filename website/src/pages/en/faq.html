Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions shopkeepers ask. Another question? Write to us.

Use

Do I need an Internet connection?

Yes. Nhill records everything on a secure server: without a connection you can neither sell nor invoice. A mobile connection (3G, 4G) is enough. If the server's answer is lost during a sale, the sale can be sent again without creating a duplicate.

Can I work in Congolese francs and US dollars?

Yes. Each document has its currency (CDF, USD, EUR…). Amounts are computed exactly, with no hidden rounding. For a document in a currency other than yours, the day's exchange rate is recorded with the document.

Is Nhill in French and English?

Yes, the interface, invoices, receipts and reports exist in both languages. Each user chooses theirs.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or Linux computer for Nhill Business and Nhill POS; Nhill Business also exists on Android tablets and phones. For the register: a USB barcode scanner (optional) and a printer for the PDF receipts.

How long does it take to start?

About half a day during the pilot: installation, import of your products from a CSV file or a spreadsheet, training of the users.

Invoices and obligations

Are Nhill invoices DGI standardised invoices?

No. Nhill is not a homologated invoicing system and is not connected to an electronic fiscal device. If you are VAT-registered, issue your standardised invoices with your device; Nhill lets you record their reference and helps you manage sales, stock and payments. Nhill documents carry the mention "management document" for VAT-registered businesses.

Does Nhill keep my accounts?

No, there is no general ledger accounting. Nhill produces the journals and exports (CSV, PDF) your accountant needs.

Data and security

Who can see my data?

You and the people you invite, according to their role. Each establishment's data is kept apart from the others. The Nhill team does not look at your customers, products or invoices; it sees overall usage figures to run the service.

What happens when an employee leaves?

You deactivate their access in Settings › Team. Their past actions stay in the activity log.

What if I forget my password?

Password reset does not exist yet in the current version; it is planned for a next version, together with e-mail sending. Until then, keep your password in a safe place or a password manager. It must have at least 8 characters: a short sentence is easy to remember.

Can I get my data back if I stop?

Yes. CSV and PDF exports are always possible, even with a suspended subscription.
