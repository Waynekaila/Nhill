Questions about the trial

Is it really free?

Yes. During the trial you pay neither for access nor for support, and no payment method is requested.

And after the trial?

You decide. The terms for continuing are presented to you in writing before the end of the trial, with a plan suited to your business. Without your agreement, access stops and you keep the export of your data.

Who sees my data?

You and the people you invite. The Nhill team does not read your invoices, customers or products; it sees overall usage figures. See the privacy policy.

Can I stop along the way?

Yes, at any time, by letting us know. You receive the export of your data.
