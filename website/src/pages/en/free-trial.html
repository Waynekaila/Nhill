Pilot programme: eight weeks to test Nhill in your business

Free, supported, no obligation to buy. We select a small number of participants so that we can follow them well.

What we provide

  • Access to Nhill Business, and to Nhill POS if you have a register, for the whole pilot.
  • Installation on your devices and import of your products.
  • Initial training for each person who will use Nhill.
  • A weekly check-in and support reachable during working hours.
  • At the end, an export of your data (CSV and PDF), whether you continue or not.

What we ask of you

  • Use Nhill for a real part of your business, preferably every working day.
  • A contact person available 20 to 30 minutes a week for the check-in.
  • Report problems and answer two short questionnaires (mid-way and at the end).
  • An Internet connection on the devices used (mobile or fixed).

How it works

  1. Application

    You fill in the form below. We answer to arrange a first conversation.

  2. Demonstration and agreement

    A demonstration on your products, then a simple pilot agreement (duration, confidentiality, data).

  3. Getting started

    Installation, catalogue import, training. Allow half a day.

  4. Eight weeks of use

    With a weekly check-in and support.

  5. Review

    What worked, what is missing, and the conditions to continue if you wish.

Who can take part

Shopkeepers, entrepreneurs and SMEs selling products or services, with at least one computer or Android tablet, who want to follow their sales, stock or cash register better. We are looking for varied businesses: shops, hardware stores, depots, pharmacies (excluding prescription medicines), service providers.

Questions about the pilot

Is it really free?

Yes. During the pilot you pay neither for access nor for support. No payment method is requested.

And after the pilot?

You decide. The conditions to continue (plan and price) are given to you in writing before the end of the pilot. Without your agreement, access stops and you keep the export of your data.

Who sees my data?

You and the people you invite. The Nhill team does not read your invoices, customers or products; it sees overall usage figures. See the privacy policy.

Can I stop part-way?

Yes, at any time, by letting us know. You receive the export of your data.
