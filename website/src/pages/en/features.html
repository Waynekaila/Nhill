What Nhill Business and Nhill POS do

This page describes the functions available in version 2.0.0. What does not exist yet is listed at the bottom of the page.

Sales and invoices

  • Quotes, purchase orders, invoices and credit notes, numbered by the server without gaps or duplicates.
  • Quote converted into an invoice, credit note on an invoice with goods returned to stock.
  • Per-line discounts and taxes (DRC profile: 16 % VAT, 0 %, exempt), exact rounding.
  • Documents in CDF, USD, EUR or another currency, with the exchange rate recorded.
  • PDF with your legal details and logo, in French or English.
  • An issued invoice can no longer be changed: it is corrected by a credit note, as in accounting.
  • For VAT-registered businesses: a field to record the reference of the standardised invoice issued by your fiscal device.

Cash register: Nhill POS

  • Each cashier signs in with their own account and only sees their register and session.
  • Opening with a float, selling by touch or with a barcode scanner (USB, works like a keyboard).
  • Payment with several methods (cash, card, Mobile Money…) and change computed.
  • Discount above the set limit: a supervisor approves with their password, valid once.
  • Returns and voids through credit notes, goods back in stock, cash refund.
  • Justified cash in and out, X reading during the day, closing with variance and Z report.
  • PDF receipts in 58 or 80 mm roll format. Keyboard shortcuts: F2 to scan, F9 to pay.

Stock, purchases and expenses

  • Products and services, references, barcodes, categories, sale and purchase prices.
  • Stock per warehouse, alert thresholds, traced movements (sale, received purchase, justified adjustment, transfer).
  • Catalogue import and export as CSV.
  • Supplier purchases: receiving goods updates the stock, amount left to pay.
  • Expenses with a category and a receipt (photo or PDF).

Payments and reports

  • Payments received and made, attached to invoices, purchases and expenses; cash and bank accounts.
  • Sales journal, payments, expenses, sales by product with estimated margin, stock movements, customer receivables, amounts to pay.
  • PDF and CSV export.

Team, access and traceability

  • Roles: owner, administrator, manager, salesperson, accountant, read-only, cash manager, cashier.
  • Employees join the business with an establishment code and an invitation; the code is renewed every 30 days.
  • Activity log: who did what and when, cannot be edited, can be exported.
  • Passwords of at least 8 characters; changing your password closes your other sessions.
  • Several establishments for the same person, with separate access to each.

What Nhill does not do yet

  • No standardised invoice homologated by the DGI (no connection to an electronic fiscal device).
  • No offline mode: an Internet connection is required.
  • No direct link with Mobile Money operators and no live online payment activated.
  • No general ledger accounting (OHADA chart of accounts): the CSV exports are for your accountant.
  • No Excel export (CSV and PDF only), no e-mail or SMS sending from the application.
  • Nhill POS and Nhill Console do not exist on Android; only Nhill Business is planned there.

These limits are public because they matter when you decide. The pilot programme also helps us choose which ones to lift first.
