Ring up fast, close right

Nhill POS is made for the counter: few actions per sale, and a closing based on figures.

  • Each cashier signs in with their own account and sees only their till and session.
  • Opening with a cash float; sales by touch or USB barcode scanner.
  • Several payment methods on one sale (cash, card, Mobile Money, bank transfer, cheque) and change worked out for you.
  • For each sale, your choice: a receipt, or an A4 invoice in the customer's name, built on the same template as in Nhill Business.
  • Discount above the limit, return or void: approval by a manager, with their own password, valid once.
  • Cash in and cash out with a reason, X reading during the day.
  • Closing: the cashier counts the drawer without seeing the expected amount; Nhill works out the difference and keeps it in the Z report.
  • PDF receipts 58 or 80 mm wide; keyboard shortcuts F2 (search and scanner) and F9 (pay).

Clean invoices, tracked credit

Your documents go out in your name, and every customer has an up-to-date balance.

  • Quotes, purchase orders, invoices and credit notes, numbered by the server with no gap or duplicate.
  • A quote becomes an invoice in one click; a credit note corrects an invoice and puts items back in stock.
  • Discounts and taxes per line, exact calculations; your tax rates are set in the settings (for example VAT 16%, 0% or exempt with the DRC profile).
  • Documents in any of the 36 available currencies, with the exchange rate recorded.
  • PDF with your logo, your identifiers and the notices your business requires, in English or French.
  • Part payments matched to the right invoice; list of what each customer owes you, per currency.
  • If you issue invoices through an approved fiscal device, a field keeps the reference of the official invoice.

Accurate stock, without constant stocktaking

Quantities only change through recorded movements: you always know why a figure changed.

  • Products and services, references, barcodes, categories, sale and purchase prices.
  • Stock per warehouse and transfers between warehouses.
  • Tracked movements: sale, purchase received, adjustment with a reason, return.
  • Alerts on products below their threshold, estimated stock value.
  • Catalogue import and export as CSV to get started quickly.
  • Supplier purchases: receiving updates the stock and keeps the amount still to pay.

Figures ready for you and your accountant

Reports are computed by the server from the recorded documents: everyone reads the same figures.

  • Sales journal, payments, expenses, sales per product with estimated margin.
  • Stock movements, customer receivables, amounts payable to suppliers.
  • Expenses by category with their receipts (photo or PDF).
  • Cash and bank accounts; payments received and made.
  • PDF and CSV export of every report.
  • Full export of your data at any time, by the account holder: an archive with CSV and JSON files and your attachments.

One team, roles, one log

Everyone works with their own access; you stay in charge of who does what.

  • Roles: owner, administrator, manager, seller, accountant, read-only, cash manager, cashier.
  • Employees join the business with the business code and an invitation; the code is renewed regularly (every 30 days by default).
  • Activity log: who did what and when, tamper-proof, exportable.
  • Forgotten password: the administrator gives the employee a temporary password, and they choose a new one at sign-in; owners and administrators receive a code by e-mail.

On your computers and phones

  • Nhill Business: Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04), Android 7 and later phones and tablets.
  • Nhill POS: Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Linux, with a USB barcode scanner if you wish.
  • Interface, documents and reports in English or French, as each user chooses.
  • An Internet connection, even mobile, is enough: an operation interrupted by a dropout is confirmed again without being counted twice.
