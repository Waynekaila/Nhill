A shop with a till and sellers

See Nhill for a shop

Your day
You are not always in the shop. In the evening, you count the money and compare it with a notebook.
With Nhill
Each seller rings up sales on Nhill POS with their own account. At closing, they count the drawer; Nhill works out the expected cash and records the difference. You check the session and the Z report in Nhill Business, even from your phone.
What reassures you
A large discount or a void needs a manager's approval, and the activity log keeps every operation.

A hardware store or depot selling at the counter and on invoice

See Nhill for a hardware store or depot

Your day
Individuals pay at the counter. Building sites and companies buy on a quote, then on an invoice, and pay in instalments, in the currency of their choice.
With Nhill
The counter goes through Nhill POS, which prints a receipt or an A4 invoice in the customer's name; business customers go through quotes, invoices and part payments in Nhill Business. Stock is the same for both, and customer receivables are shown per currency.
What saves you time
Catalogue import as CSV at the start, low-stock alerts before a stock-out, transfers between warehouses.

A service provider or a sole trader

See Nhill for a service provider

Your day
Quotes and invoices made in a word processor, payments tracked from memory.
With Nhill
A simple space for one person: quotes and invoices as PDF in your name, payments received, expenses with receipts, and the list of what each customer owes you. On a computer or an Android phone.

An SME with several employees and an accountant

See Nhill for an SME

Your day
Several people sell and buy; the accountant receives the paperwork late.
With Nhill
Each employee has a suitable role: seller, manager, accountant who reads the reports. Reports download as CSV and PDF, and the activity log shows who issued, collected or changed what.
What helps you grow
Several tills, several warehouses, and employees you add as you hire, according to your plan.
