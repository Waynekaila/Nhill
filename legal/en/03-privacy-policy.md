## 1. Who is responsible

**Nhill Solution**, with its head office in Kinshasa (Democratic Republic of the Congo), is
the controller of the processing described here. Contact for any question about your
data: info@nhill.online.

This policy covers: visitors to the nhill.online website, people who write to us or fill
in a form, users of the Nhill Business, Nhill POS and Nhill Console applications, and
people who contact support.

It does not cover the data each business records in Nhill about its own customers,
suppliers and employees: the business is responsible for it and Nhill Solution processes
it on its behalf, as a processor (data processing agreement, document 06).

We do not sell personal data and do no advertising profiling. The website sets **no
cookie** and uses no audience measurement tool (document 04).

## 3. Recipients

- the members of the Nhill Solution team who handle your request or provide support,
  within the limits of their duties;
- our host, Hostinger International Ltd (61 Lordou Vironos str., 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus),
  which provides the server of the Nhill service, located in France;
- the provider that delivers our e-mails, for the messages we send you;
- the payment provider the business uses to pay its subscription, for that payment only;
- authorities, on lawful request.

We do not pass your data on to any advertiser.

## 4. Where the data is

The data of the Nhill service is hosted on a server located in France, in the European
Union, and backed up every day in encrypted form. The Nhill Solution team, based in
Kinshasa, accesses it remotely, over encrypted connections, for operations and support.
For a person or business located in the DRC, this hosting is a transfer of data outside
the country.

## 5. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), each business's data kept separate from the others' in the
database, passwords stored as fingerprints, mandatory two-factor authentication for
platform administrators, limited and logged team access, encrypted backups. Summary:
document 13. In case of a data breach that presents a risk, we inform the people and
businesses concerned (procedure: document 14).

## 6. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, erasure or portability, the
restriction of its use, object to its use and withdraw at any time your agreement to
receive news, by writing to info@nhill.online (optional form: document 19). We answer
within one month. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask you to
confirm it.

If you use Nhill within a business, first send your request to that business: it decides
how its data is used, and we help it answer you.

You can also contact the competent data protection authority of your country, for example,
if you live in the European Union, that of your Member State.

## 7. Employees of businesses

Nhill's activity log records the actions of each user in the application. The employing
business is responsible for this processing and informs its staff (model notice:
document 17).

## 8. Minors

The Service is intended for professionals; it is not designed for children.

## 9. Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes; the version date appears at the top of
the text. Significant changes are notified to subscribing businesses.
