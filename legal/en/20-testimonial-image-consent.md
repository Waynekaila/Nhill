## 1. Rules

- **One consent per use.** The exact text, photo, video or figure is attached to the form;
  the person reads it before signing. A new edit or a changed text needs a new consent.
- **Separate and free consent.** Refusing or withdrawing has no effect on the trial, the
  subscription or support.
- **Each recognisable person signs for themselves.** The owner's consent covers the
  business's name and logo, not the image or voice of an employee or of a customer present
  in the shop.
- **No images of minors.** Nobody under 18 is filmed or photographed, even in the
  background.
- **Results as measured.** A figure (closing time, till difference, stock difference) is
  published only with its period and measurement method, approved by the business.
- **Visible consideration.** If the business receives something in return (discount, gift,
  service), it is stated on the form and mentioned next to the published testimonial.
- **Withdrawal at any time.** On a written request to info@nhill.online, Nhill Solution
  removes the content from the media it manages (website, social networks, digital
  documents) as soon as possible and makes no new use of it. Printed documents already
  handed out cannot be recalled.
- **Retention.** The signed form is kept while the content is used, then until the end of
  the period during which a claim can be made (point V-07); it is filed in the
  administrative records, not in the prospect tracker.
