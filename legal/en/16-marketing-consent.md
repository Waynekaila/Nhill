## 1. Rules

- **No pre-ticked box**, no agreement inferred from silence or from a business card left
  behind.
- One agreement **per channel**: e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp, call.
- Agreement separate from the demo request: refusing news does not prevent being called
  back for the requested demo.
- At most one news message a month.
- Every commercial message names the sender and a simple way to unsubscribe ("STOP" on
  WhatsApp or SMS, link or reply in the e-mail); unsubscribing is applied without waiting
  for the next message.
- WhatsApp: add only people who agreed; prefer broadcast lists (recipients do not see the
  other numbers).
- Never buy or rent a contact list.

## 3. Register of agreements

Agreements collected outside the website are entered in the register the same day, with
the following columns:

| Date and time | Person (name, organisation) | Channel | Contact detail | Wording shown (version) | Source (fair, meeting, partner, call) | Proof (form, screenshot, message) | Withdrawal (date, means) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The website form records the date, the choice and the policy version automatically
(`consent_marketing` column of the website database).

## 4. Partners

A partner passes on only contacts who agreed to be contacted by Nhill Solution
(document 10, article 6), with the corresponding proof.
