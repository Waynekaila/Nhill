## Prohibited uses

Nhill must not be used to:

1. try to access another business's data, bypass access controls, or test security without
   the Publisher's written authorisation;
2. deliberately overload the Service (massive automated requests, unauthorised bots);
3. share an account between several people, or hand access to a third party;
4. record unlawful content, unlawfully obtained data, or sensitive data (health, opinions,
   biometric data) without a legal basis;
5. issue false documents, hide transactions or defraud the tax administration;
6. send unsolicited commercial messages from the Service's features;
7. resell the Service or make it available to third parties without written agreement (see
   the partnership agreement, template 10);
8. decompile the applications beyond what the law allows.

## Vulnerability reporting

Anyone who finds a flaw reports it to [SECURITY E-MAIL] without exploiting or disclosing it
before it is fixed. The Publisher undertakes to [acknowledge receipt within 72 hours].

## Measures

In case of breach, the Publisher may, depending on severity: warn, suspend a user account,
suspend the business (read-only) or terminate the contract, after informing the Customer
except in an emergency (security, fraud, request from an authority). Every platform action
on a business is justified and written to the audit log.
