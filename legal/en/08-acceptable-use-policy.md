## Prohibited uses

Nhill may not be used to:

1. try to access another business's data, bypass access controls or test security
   without the Publisher's written permission;
2. deliberately overload the Service (massive automated requests, unauthorised bots);
3. share an account between several people, or give access to a third party;
4. record unlawful content, unlawfully obtained data, or sensitive data (health, opinions,
   biometric data) without a legal basis;
5. issue false documents, conceal transactions or defraud a tax administration;
6. send unsolicited commercial messages from the Service's functions;
7. resell or make the Service available to third parties without the Publisher's written
   agreement (document 10);
8. decompile the applications beyond what the law allows.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Anyone who discovers a flaw reports it to support@nhill.online (subject "Security")
without exploiting or disclosing it before it is fixed. The Publisher acknowledges the
report and keeps the person informed of its handling.

## Measures

In case of breach, the Publisher may, depending on its seriousness: warn, suspend a user
account, suspend the business (read-only) or end the contract, after warning the Customer
except in an emergency (security, fraud, request from an authority). Every action of the
platform on a business is justified and recorded in the audit log.
