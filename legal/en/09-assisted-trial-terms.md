## 1. Purpose

Let a business (the "Business") use Nhill free of charge for 30 days, with the support of
**Nhill Solution** (the "Publisher"), to check that the software suits its activity before
any decision.

## 2. What is included

- access to Nhill Business and, if the Business has a till, to Nhill POS, within the limits
  of the trial plan shown in the application;
- a demonstration on the Business's products;
- installation on its computers and phones, remotely or on site in Kinshasa, and import of
  its catalogue from a file it provides;
- initial training for each person who will use Nhill, according to their role;
- a check-in every week and support through the "Help and support" menu and by e-mail at
  support@nhill.online;
- at the end, the full export of the data, whether or not the Business continues.

## 3. Free of charge, no commitment

The trial is free: neither the access nor the support is charged, and no payment details
are asked for. The Business commits to no purchase.

## 4. What the Business undertakes to do

Use Nhill for a real part of its activity; appoint a contact person available 20 to 30
minutes a week; share its feedback on what works and what is missing.

## 5. End of the trial

5.1 Before the end of the trial, the Publisher presents in writing the conditions for
continuing, with a suitable plan. The Business pays only after accepting a proposal.

5.2 Without an accepted proposal, access switches to read-only at the end of the trial:
the data can still be viewed and exported for 90 days, then is deleted.

5.3 Either party can stop the trial at any time by informing the other in writing; the
Business keeps the export of its data.

## 6. Tax documents

Nhill **is not an invoicing device approved or standardised by a tax administration**.
During the trial, the Business keeps its usual records and, if it is required to, keeps
using its approved fiscal device.

## 7. Liability

As the trial is free, the Publisher is liable only for proven direct damage caused by its
gross negligence or wilful misconduct, subject to the applicable mandatory rules (the cap
of article 10.3 of document 02, calculated on the amounts paid, is nil during a free
trial).

## 8. Data

The Publisher processes the Business's data as processor, under the data processing
agreement (document 06), which applies during the trial. The Business informs its staff
about the activity log (document 17).

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, for as long as it has
not become public through no fault of its own.

## 10. Name, feedback and testimonials

10.1 The Business's feedback may be used freely to improve Nhill.

10.2 The Publisher cites the Business's name, its logo or a testimonial **only with its
prior written agreement, given separately for each use**, on a text the Business has read
and approved. No figure on results is published without this agreement.

## 11. Governing law

Law of the Democratic Republic of the Congo; search for an amicable solution, then the
competent courts of Kinshasa, without depriving the Business of the protection of the
mandatory rules of its country.
