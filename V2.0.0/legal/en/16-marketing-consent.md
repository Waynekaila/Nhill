## 1. Rules

- **No pre-ticked box**, no consent inferred from silence or from a business card left
  behind.
- One consent **per channel**: e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp, call.
- Consent separate from the demo request: refusing news does not prevent being called back
  for the requested demo.
- Every commercial message identifies the sender and a simple way to unsubscribe ("STOP" on
  WhatsApp/SMS, link in the e-mail); unsubscription is applied within [48] hours.
- WhatsApp groups and broadcast lists: only add people who consented; prefer broadcast lists
  (recipients do not see the other numbers).
- Never buy or rent a contact list.

## 2. Form wording (paper or online)

> **Receive news from Nhill**
>
> [COMPANY NAME] (Nhill) would like to send you information about its offers, new features
> and workshops. Tick the channels you accept:
>
> ☐ E-mail: ____________________  ☐ WhatsApp: ____________________  ☐ SMS
>
> You can withdraw your agreement at any time: reply "STOP" or write to [DPO E-MAIL]. Your
> details are not passed to third parties. Details: privacy policy, [ADDRESS].
>
> Name: ____________ Organisation: ____________ Date: ________ Signature: ________

The website form automatically records the date, the choice and the policy version
(`consent_marketing`, website database). Consents collected offline are entered in this
register the same day.

## 4. Partners

A partner only passes on contacts who agreed to be contacted by Nhill (template 10,
article 8), with the corresponding proof.
