## Between

**[PUBLISHER COMPANY NAME]**, RCCM [RCCM], represented by [NAME, TITLE] (the "Publisher"),
and **[PILOT BUSINESS NAME]**, [FORM], RCCM [RCCM or "not registered"], [ADDRESS],
represented by [NAME, TITLE] (the "Pilot").

## 1. Purpose

Test Nhill (Nhill Business, and Nhill POS if useful) in the Pilot's real conditions, collect
its feedback and measure the results agreed in article 4.

## 2. Term

From [START DATE] to [END DATE] ([8 to 12] weeks). Extension possible by written agreement,
including by e-mail.

## 3. Conditions

3.1 Access is **free** during the pilot, for at most [N] users and [N] tills. No purchase
commitment is required. **[H-16]**

3.2 At the end of the pilot, the Pilot may subscribe [at the public price / with the "pilot"
discount of [N] % for [N] months]; otherwise its data remains exportable for [90] days, then
is deleted.

3.3 The Publisher provides: a [2]-hour onboarding session (on site in Kinshasa or remote),
the initial import of products from a file provided by the Pilot, priority support through
[BUSINESS WHATSAPP / E-MAIL] during business hours.

3.4 The Pilot undertakes to: use Nhill for [counter sales / invoicing / stock] during the
pilot; appoint a contact person; take part in [3] 30-minute check-ins; answer the
end-of-pilot questionnaire.

## 5. Pilot version and liability

5.1 The Pilot knows it is using a version under evaluation: anomalies may occur. It keeps
its usual records (book, standardised invoices) during the pilot.

5.2 Nhill **is not a certified invoicing system**: a VAT-registered Pilot keeps issuing
standardised invoices with a certified device. **[TO VALIDATE: V-12.]**

5.3 The Publisher's liability is limited to direct damage caused by its proven fault, up to
[AMOUNT] **[TO VALIDATE: V-01 — cap for a free service]**.

## 6. Data

The Publisher processes the Pilot's data as processor (data processing agreement, template
06, applicable during the pilot). The Pilot informs its staff of the activity log
(template 17).

## 7. Feedback, name and testimonial

7.1 The Pilot's feedback may be used freely to improve Nhill.

7.2 The Publisher only cites the Pilot's name, logo or a testimonial **with its prior
written consent, given separately** for each use (website, presentation, social media).
Result figures are only published with this consent and as measured, without favourable
rounding.
