Nhill pilot programme

Eight weeks to test Nhill in your business. Free, supported, no purchase commitment.

What we provide

  • Nhill Business, and Nhill POS if you have a till
  • Installation on your devices and import of your products
  • Training for each user
  • A weekly check-in and support during working hours
  • At the end, the export of your data (CSV, PDF)

What we ask

  • Use Nhill for a real part of your business
  • A contact person, 20 to 30 minutes a week
  • Report problems, answer two short questionnaires
  • A Windows or Linux computer, or an Android 7+ tablet, and Internet

How it works

  1. 1  Application
  2. 2  Demo with your products and pilot agreement
  3. 3  Onboarding: half a day
  4. 4  Eight weeks of use, a weekly check-in
  5. 5  Review, and written terms to continue if you wish
