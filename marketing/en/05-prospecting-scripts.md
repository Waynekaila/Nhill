# Prospecting scripts (shop visit, call, WhatsApp voice note)

*Français : [../fr/05-scripts-prospection.md](../fr/05-scripts-prospection.md)*

Principles: short, polite, concrete; talk about the trader's problem before the software;
promise nothing Nhill does not do; ask for consent before recording a number for messages
(template `legal/en/16-marketing-consent.md`). Adapt the language: French, Lingala or English
depending on the person. Most visits in Kinshasa will be in French or Lingala; these English
versions serve English-speaking prospects and partners.

## 1. Shop visit (2 minutes)

> Good morning [Madam / Sir], my name is [FIRST NAME], from Nhill. I'll only take two
> minutes — I can see you have customers.
>
> We help traders in [COMMUNE] keep track of their sales, stock and till on a computer, a
> tablet or a phone. For example, in the evening, you see how much each seller collected,
> without redoing the notebook sums.
>
> Does the evening till take you time, or do you sometimes have differences?
>
> *[Listen. Ask 2 or 3 discovery questions, see `04` § 4.]*
>
> We offer a 30-day assisted free trial, with installation and training. I can
> show you in fifteen minutes, with your own products, when you're less busy. Which day
> suits you?

**If "I don't have time"**: "I understand. Can I come back [day] at [time]? Or I'll leave
you this sheet, and if you allow me to write to you on WhatsApp, I'll send a short video."

**If "it's expensive"**: "During the pilot it's free and without commitment. Prices will be
set with the pilot traders and published before any invoicing."

**If "I'm VAT-registered"**: "Nhill doesn't replace your DGI standardised-invoice device. It
helps with stock, the till and tracking sellers; you keep your device for the tax invoice."

**If "what if the Internet cuts?"**: "Nhill needs the Internet to record. If the connection
drops in the middle of a sale, you retry it and it isn't counted twice. For a long outage,
keep a backup notebook; we enter it afterwards."

**If "my data?"**: "It's yours. Each business is kept separate from the others in the
database, and our team doesn't see your invoices or customers. You can export everything
whenever you want."

## 2. Phone call (after a referral)

> Hello [Madam / Sir], [FIRST NAME] from Nhill. [REFERRER'S NAME] gave me your number and
> said you might be interested in a tool to track the till and stock. Do you have two
> minutes?
>
> *[If yes]* How do you manage the till today? … I suggest a fifteen-minute demo at your
> shop, with your products. Tuesday or Thursday?
>
> *[If no]* May I send you a 60-second video on WhatsApp, if you agree? *[Record the
> consent, date, channel.]*

## 3. WhatsApp voice note (30 seconds, only with consent)

> Hello [FIRST NAME], it's [FIRST NAME] from Nhill, thanks for agreeing. As promised: Nhill
> lets you see your sales, your stock and each seller's till, even when you're not in the
> shop. I'll send the video right after. If you'd like the free demo with your products,
> just reply with a day that suits you. Have a good day.

## 4. After the contact

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Enter the contact in `trackers/suivi-prospects.csv` (status, score, consent) | same day |
| Send what was promised (video, sheet) | within 24 h |
| Follow up if no answer | D+3, then D+10, then stop |
