# 30-second and 2-minute pitches

*Français : [../fr/13-pitchs.md](../fr/13-pitchs.md)*

## 1. 30-second pitch (≈ 75 words)

> Many traders in Kinshasa end the day with a till notebook that doesn't add up, without
> knowing what's left in stock or what each seller collected.
>
> Nhill is simple management software: every sale is recorded under the cashier's name, the
> stock follows, and the owner sees everything from their phone, even when away.
>
> We're running a free eight-week pilot programme, with installation and training. May I
> show it to you with your products in fifteen minutes?

## 2. 2-minute pitch (≈ 300 words)

> **The problem.** In a 2025 survey of 914 small Congolese businesses, half already use
> digital tools, almost all a smartphone — but only one in five of those equipped uses stock
> software. The till, the stock and control of sellers stay on paper. The result:
> differences nobody can explain, stock-outs found too late, and an owner who can never be
> away.
>
> **What Nhill does.** Nhill brings three applications onto one server. Nhill POS for the
> till: sell fast, several payment methods, discounts approved by a supervisor, a closing
> where the cashier counts without seeing the expected amount. Nhill Business for the rest:
> stock through movements, purchases, expenses, invoices and credit sales, per-employee
> reports, and an activity log nobody can erase. All in French and English, in Congolese
> francs and dollars, on computers, and on Android tablets or phones for Nhill Business.
>
> **What sets us apart.** Nhill is built for local reality: an operation cut by a network
> outage is retried without being counted twice; each business is isolated from the others
> in the database; and we provide on-site support in Kinshasa.
>
> **What Nhill is not.** It is not standardised-invoice software certified by the DGI: VAT
> payers keep their tax device and use Nhill for management.
>
> **Where we are.** Version 2.0 is built and tested; we are starting a free eight-week pilot
> with about ten traders, to set prices from measured results.
>
> **What we ask.** [For a trader:] fifteen minutes for a demo with your products. [For a
> partner:] introduce us to traders in your network, or host a workshop for your members.
