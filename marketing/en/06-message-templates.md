# Message templates (WhatsApp, e-mail)

*Français : [../fr/06-modeles-messages.md](../fr/06-modeles-messages.md)*

Common rules: only send to someone who agreed to this channel, or who wrote first; sign with
a first name; one follow-up at a time; unsubscribe mention in commercial messages. Replace
the `[BRACKETS]`.

## 1. WhatsApp — after a visit (consent given)

> Hello [FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR FIRST NAME] from Nhill, we met today at [SHOP NAME]. Thank
> you for your welcome.
> Here is the short video I promised: [LINK]. For the free demo with your products (15 min),
> just tell me which day suits you.
> To stop receiving messages from Nhill, reply STOP.

## 2. WhatsApp — demo confirmation

> Hello [FIRST NAME], I confirm our demo on [DAY] at [TIME] at [PLACE]. If you can, prepare
> a list of the 10 to 20 items you sell most: I'll show you Nhill with them. See you [DAY]!

## 3. WhatsApp — follow-up (D+3, once only)

> Hello [FIRST NAME], I'm getting back to you about Nhill. Would you still like to see the
> demo? If it's not the right time, no problem — just tell me and I won't follow up again.

## 4. E-mail — first contact after a website form

**Subject**: Your Nhill demo request

> Hello [FIRST NAME],
>
> Thank you for requesting a Nhill demo for [ORGANISATION].
>
> I suggest 30 minutes, at your shop or by video call, with your own products. Two slots:
> [SLOT 1] or [SLOT 2]. If neither suits you, suggest another.
>
> To prepare, could you tell me: how many people sell, which devices you have (computer,
> Android tablet), and what you'd most like to improve (till, stock, credit sales)?
>
> Kind regards,
> [FIRST NAME LAST NAME] — Nhill
> info@nhill.online · nhill.online

## 5. E-mail — meeting request to an organisation (association, incubator)

**Subject**: Meeting proposal — a management tool for your trader members

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am [FIRST NAME LAST NAME], from Nhill, sales, stock and till management software
> designed for traders and SMEs in the DRC (French and English, CDF and USD).
>
> We offer in Kinshasa a 30-day assisted free trial, with installation and
> training. It may interest members of [ORGANISATION] who want to keep better track of their
> till and stock.
>
> Would you agree to a 30-minute conversation [this week / next week] to see whether a
> collaboration makes sense? I can come to your offices.
>
> Important: Nhill is management software; it is not certified by the DGI for the
> standardised invoice.
>
> Kind regards,
> [FIRST NAME LAST NAME] — Nhill Solution — info@nhill.online

## 6. Collaboration proposal (after the meeting)

**Subject**: Nhill × [ORGANISATION] — proposal

> Hello [FIRST NAME],
>
> Thank you for our conversation on [DATE]. As agreed, here is our proposal:
>
> 1. **A 90-minute workshop** for your members: keeping track of your till and stock, with a
>    Nhill demo. Proposed date: [DATE].
> 2. **Reserved places** in the assisted free trial for [N] of your members.
> 3. **[If a partnership is agreed]** a [N] % commission on your members' subscriptions for
>    12 months, or a [N] % discount for them — to be set in a written contract.
>
> What we ask: share the workshop invitation with your members (without passing us their
> details without their consent).
>
> Happy to discuss.
> [FIRST NAME LAST NAME] — Nhill

## 7. Referral request (satisfied pilot, week 9)

> Hello [FIRST NAME], thank you again for taking part in the pilot. You told me that
> [BENEFIT MENTIONED BY THE PILOT]. Do you know one or two traders Nhill could help in the
> same way? If so, ask them first whether they agree, then send me their name and number, or
> give them our address: info@nhill.online.
