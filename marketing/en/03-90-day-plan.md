# 90-day launch plan — Kinshasa

*Français : [../fr/03-plan-90-jours.md](../fr/03-plan-90-jours.md)*

| | |
|---|---|
| Period | 13 weeks from the start date chosen by the team (W1 = first week; the Monday of W1 is entered in the tracking workbook, "Tableau de bord" sheet) |
| Goal | 10 active pilots, 3 signed partners, first paid subscriptions after the pilot reviews |
| Conversion | **1 USD = 2,264.0000 CDF**, official rate of the [Central Bank of the Congo](https://www.bcc.cd/marche-des-changes/cours-de-change) on **26/10/2026**; CDF amounts rounded to the unit. Recompute at the day's rate before any spending |
| Prerequisites | legal notice, terms and privacy policy published (3.0 website, `legal/` folder), deliverables checked (`release/V3.0.0/`) |

> The amounts below are **proposed budget envelopes**, not recorded prices: each line is
> confirmed by a quote or receipt, logged in the spending tracker
> (`trackers/kpi-tableau-de-bord.xlsx`, "Budget" sheet).

| Item | USD | CDF |

| Mobile data bundles for demos (3 months) | 45 | 101,880 |

Channels: targeted door-to-door, WhatsApp Business (free app), pilots' word of mouth,
referral partners paid by commission (no fixed cost).

## 2. Week-by-week plan

| Wk | Theme | Actions (A = scenario A, B = in addition for scenario B) | Indicator of the week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preparation | WhatsApp Business account (catalogue, greeting, hours); trial sheets printed; list of 60 target businesses in 3 communes (Gombe, Limete, Ngaliema) in the prospect tracker; rehearsed demo (script `07`) — B: roll-up and cards | 60 prospects listed |
| 2 | First contacts | 30 door-to-door visits (script `05`); aim for 10 demo requests; 5 support organisations contacted (message template `06`) | 30 contacts, 8 demos booked |
| 3 | Demos | 8 demos with the prospect's products; report after each demo; pilot selection (grid `04`) — B: ads launched (to the "Free trial" form) | 8 demos, 4 pilots selected |
| 4 | Pilot wave 1 | trial terms accepted; onboarding of 4–6 pilots (half a day each); baseline measurements | 5 pilots installed |
| 5 | Close follow-up | 2 short calls per pilot; problems fixed; 20 new visits | 0 blocking incident open > 48 h |
| 6 | Partners | 2 partner meetings (integrator, trainer, association); partnership agreement (template `legal/10`) — B: 1st partner training | 2 partners in discussion |
| 7 | Pilot wave 2 | 4–6 new pilots (including partner referrals); mid-pilot questionnaire wave 1 | 10 pilots in total |
| 8 | Content | 30 s video filmed at a business **with its written consent** (script `11`, form `legal/en/20`); posted on WhatsApp Status and social media — B: entrepreneurs' event (live demo) | video published |
| 9 | Referrals | referral request to satisfied pilots (template `06` § 7); group demo (A: lent room) | 5 referrals |
| 10 | Wave 2 mid-point | questionnaire; interim measurements; pricing proposal prepared | pilot activity rate |
| 11 | Wave 1 review | end-of-pilot reviews; written follow-up proposal (prices set according to `01` § 6) | first pilot decisions |
| 12 | First subscriptions | conversion of satisfied pilots; invoicing (according to the validated tax position); partner review — B: 2nd partner training | first paid subscriptions |
| 13 | Review | KPI review (`10`); decision: wide launch, second pilot wave or fixes; plan for the next 90 days | 90-day report |

## 3. Time split (team of 2)

| Activity | Share of time |
|---|---|
| Field and WhatsApp prospecting | 35 % |
| Demos and onboarding | 30 % |
| Pilot follow-up and support | 25 % |
| Content, partners, administration | 10 % |

## 4. Rules

- Every promise made to a prospect already exists in the software (no "coming soon"
  feature sold as available).
- No commercial message without consent (template `legal/16`).
- No business name, logo or testimonial without separate written consent (trial terms,
  article 10; form `legal/en/20`).
- Every expense is logged with its receipt; a > 20 % overrun on an item → explicit decision.
