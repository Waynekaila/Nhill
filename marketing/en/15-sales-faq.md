# Sales FAQ (answers for the team and partners)

*Français : [../fr/15-faq-commerciale.md](../fr/15-faq-commerciale.md)*

Validated answers for version 3.0.0, aligned with the website (`website/src/pages/en/faq.html`)
and the [compliance matrix](../../docs/en/website-compliance-matrix.md). If a question isn't
here, don't improvise: "I'll check and answer you in writing."

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How much does it cost? | Nhill is a subscription per business: users, tills and warehouses are included according to the plan. No price is published: you start with the 30-day assisted free trial, then the business receives a written proposal (plan, term, price, currency, payment methods). |
| Does it comply with the DGI? | Nhill is management software. It is **not an approved or standardised invoicing device**. If you must issue standardised invoices, keep your approved device for your tax invoices; Nhill lets you record their reference for reconciliation. |
| Does it work without Internet? | No: Nhill needs a connection to record; a mobile connection is enough. A sale cut by an outage can be validated again without being counted twice. For a long outage, a fallback book and entry afterwards. |
| On which devices? | Nhill Business: Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04), Android 7 and later phones and tablets. Nhill POS: Windows and Linux, with a USB barcode reader if you wish. |
| Do I need a special printer? | No. The receipt is a 58 or 80 mm PDF, printable on a receipt printer or a regular printer; the A4 invoice prints on a regular printer. |
| Can the till issue an invoice? | Yes: for each sale, the cashier chooses a receipt or an A4 invoice in the customer's name, identical to the Nhill Business one (same numbering, same notices). |
| Is my data safe? | Each business is kept separate from the others in the database; encrypted connections; passwords never stored in clear; activity log that cannot be changed; encrypted backups every day. No software is flawless: we have not had an external audit yet, it is planned. |
| Who sees my data? | You and the people you invite, according to their role. The Nhill team does not see your invoices, customers or products; only overall usage figures, and each consultation is recorded in your log. |
| Where is the data hosted? | On a server located in France, in the European Union, rented from the host Hostinger; encrypted backups every day. The team accesses it remotely from Kinshasa, over encrypted connections. |
| Can I get my data back? | Yes, at any time: CSV and PDF exports, even in read-only mode; the account holder downloads the full archive. At the end of the contract, 90 days to export, then deletion. |
| How many users? | According to the business's plan; during the trial, within the limits shown in the application. Each person has their own account. |
| I forgot my password. | Employee: the business's administrator gives you a temporary password (Settings › Team), to replace at the next login. Owner or administrator: "Forgot password?" sends a one-time code by e-mail. |
| Must the password be complicated? | At least 8 characters, not a too-common password. A short phrase is easy to remember. No obligation to change it regularly. |
| Do you handle several shops? | Several tills and, depending on the plan, several warehouses with stock transfers. |
| What about accounting? | Nhill produces the sales, payments and expenses journals, receivables and payables, exportable in CSV and PDF for your accountant. It is not general accounting software. |
| Is Mobile Money integrated? | You record a Mobile Money payment (amount, reference) next to cash, card, transfer and cheque; Nhill does not drive the operators. |
| Can I send an invoice to the customer? | The invoice is a PDF in your name; you attach it to an e-mail or a message. On Android, the "Share" button sends it straight to messaging or e-mail. |
| Several currencies? | Yes: each document has its currency, among 36 currencies (USD, EUR, CDF, XAF, XOF, NGN, KES, ZAR…), with the day's rate recorded. Totals stay presented currency by currency. |
| Who are you? | Nhill Solution, publisher of Nhill, based in Kinshasa. We support each business from the demonstration to the first weeks of use. Contact: info@nhill.online; support: support@nhill.online. |
| Can I become a partner? | Yes: referral, installation and support, training, support of entrepreneurs. Conditions are set in writing before starting. "Partners" page of the website. |
