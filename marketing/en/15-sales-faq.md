# Sales FAQ (answers for the team and partners)

*Français : [../fr/15-faq-commerciale.md](../fr/15-faq-commerciale.md)*

Validated answers for version 2.0.0. If a question isn't here, don't improvise: "I'll check
and get back to you by [time]."

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How much does it cost? | During the pilot programme: free, no payment method. Prices will be set from the pilot results and published, in CDF and USD, before any invoicing. |
| Is it DGI-compliant? | Nhill is management software. It is **not certified** for the standardised invoice. If you are VAT-registered, keep your DGI device for tax invoices; Nhill lets you record its reference for reconciliation. |
| Does it work without the Internet? | No: Nhill needs a connection to record. An operation cut by an outage is retried without being counted twice. For a long outage, a backup notebook, then entry afterwards. |
| On which devices? | Nhill Business: Windows 10/11 64-bit, Linux (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04), Android 7 and later. Nhill POS: Windows and Linux. *(Check the available deliverables before answering.)* |
| Do I need a special printer? | No. The receipt is a 58 or 80 mm PDF printable on a receipt printer or a standard printer. |
| Is my data safe? | Each business is isolated from the others in the database; encrypted communications; passwords never stored in plain text; audit log. No software is flawless: we haven't had an external audit yet; it is planned. |
| Who sees my data? | You and the people you invite. The Nhill team doesn't see your invoices or customers; only overall usage figures, and each view is written to your log. |
| Where is the data hosted? | [To complete once the host and region are chosen — see `docs/en/legal-parameters.md`.] |
| Can I get my data back? | Yes, at any time: CSV and PDF exports. At the end of the pilot or subscription, you keep the export. |
| How many users? | As many accounts as the plan allows; during the pilot, [N]. Each person has their own account. |
| I forgot my password. | Employee: the establishment administrator gives you a temporary password (Settings › Team), to replace at the next sign-in. Owner or administrator: "Forgot your password?" e-mails a one-time code (version 2.1, SMTP relay configured by the operator). |
| Must the password be complicated? | At least 8 characters, not a very common password. A short sentence is easy to remember. No obligation to change it regularly. |
| Do you handle several shops? | Several tills and, depending on the plan, several warehouses with stock transfers. |
| What about accounting? | Nhill produces sales journals, payments, expenses, receivables and payables, exportable for your accountant. It is not general accounting software. |
| Is Mobile Money payment built in? | You record a Mobile Money payment (amount, reference); Nhill does not connect to the operators. |
| Who are you? | [Presentation of the team and the operating company, with RCCM — to complete.] |
| Can I become a partner? | Yes: referral, installation, training, support. Terms in a written contract. "Partners" page of the website. |
